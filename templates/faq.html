Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about ChatOnline

Getting Started

How does ChatOnline work?
ChatOnline connects you with strangers from around the world. You can use random chat to be matched with random people, join chat rooms on specific topics, or explore dating channels. No registration is required - just enter your basic info and start chatting!
Is ChatOnline free?
Yes! ChatOnline is 100% free. There are no hidden fees, premium tiers, or paid features. Everyone gets full access to all features including random chat, chat rooms, friends, messaging, and more.
Do I need to register?
No registration is required! You can start chatting immediately as a guest. However, creating an account lets you save your profile, add friends, and access your chat history across devices.
How old do I need to be?
ChatOnline is available for users aged 18 and older. If you're under 18, you'll need to get verified through a parent or guardian. We take safety seriously, especially for younger users.

Chat Features

How does random chat work?
Random chat instantly connects you with a stranger. You can set your gender preference (male, female, or everyone) and our system will find someone for you. If you don't like the person, just click "Next" to find someone new. It's that simple!
What are chat rooms?
Chat rooms are public spaces where multiple users can chat together on specific topics. We have rooms for different interests like gaming, music, movies, dating, and more. Anyone can join and participate in the conversation.
Can I use ChatOnline on my phone?
Absolutely! ChatOnline works great on mobile devices. Our website is fully responsive and adapts to any screen size. You can chat on your phone, tablet, or desktop - your conversation syncs across all devices.

Safety & Privacy

Is my chat private?
Your conversations are private between you and the person you're chatting with. We don't monitor or record your private chats. However, please remember that strangers are... strangers. Don't share personal information like your address, phone number, or financial details.
How do I report someone?
If someone behaves inappropriately, you can report them by clicking the report button in the chat interface. Our moderation team reviews all reports and takes action against users who violate our community guidelines. You can also block users to prevent further contact.
What are the community rules?
We expect all users to be respectful. Don't harass, bully, or spam others. Don't share explicit content or engage in illegal activities. Don't impersonate others or collect personal information. Violating rules can result in your account being banned.

Account & Technical

How do I create an account?
Click "Sign Up" on the homepage. You'll need to provide a username, email, password, and basic info. That's it! You can also continue as a guest without creating an account.
What if I'm not matched with anyone?
If random chat takes too long, try changing your gender preference or checking back during peak hours (typically evenings and weekends). You can also try chat rooms or dating channels for more immediate connections.
The page isn't loading properly. What should I do?
Try these steps: 1) Refresh the page, 2) Clear your browser cache, 3) Disable browser extensions temporarily, 4) Try a different browser. If the problem persists, it might be a temporary server issue - try again in a few minutes.
