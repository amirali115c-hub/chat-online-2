🛡️ Stay Safe While Chatting

Your safety is our priority. Learn how to protect yourself online

Basic Safety Guidelines

Never Share Personal Information

Don't give out your real name, address, phone number, email, school, or workplace to strangers. Scammers often ask for these details early in conversations.

Never Send Money or Gifts

Never send money, gift cards, or valuable items to people you meet online. Scammers often build trust before asking for money.

Be Careful with Photos & Video

Think twice before sharing photos or enabling webcam. Your images can be saved, screenshots, or used against you. Keep your face private until you trust someone.

Don't Believe Everything You Hear

People can lie about who they are. Be cautious of people who seem too perfect, ask personal questions early, or make excuses not to video chat.

Warning Signs

Watch out for these red flags:

  • Asks for your personal info (name, address, phone, school)
  • Claims to be from another country but asks for money
  • Says they can't video chat despite having a camera
  • Asks you to move to another platform (WhatsApp, Telegram, etc.)
  • Talks about "investment opportunities" or "easy money"
  • Asks you to click suspicious links or download files
  • Gives you a sad story to get your sympathy and money
  • Asks for gift cards or cryptocurrency

How to Protect Yourself

Block Bad Users

If someone makes you uncomfortable, immediately click the Block button. You don't owe them an explanation. Your peace of mind matters more.

Report Problem Users

Use the Report button to tell us about bad behavior. Our moderation team reviews every report. Help us keep the community safe!

Verify with Video

If someone claims to be who they say, ask to video chat. Real people will verify. Refusal to video is a major red flag.

Take Your Time

Don't rush into sharing anything. Real connections build over time. Anyone pressuring you for personal info or photos is suspicious.

For Younger Users

Important for ages 13-17:

  • Never meet someone from online in person - People can pretend to be your age
  • Tell a parent if someone makes you uncomfortable - No matter what they say
  • Don't share your school name or location - Even your city can be too much
  • If something feels wrong, it probably is - Trust your instincts!
