Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. Learn how we protect and handle your data.

Last Updated: March 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect minimal information to provide our services:

  • Account Data: Username, email, password (encrypted), gender, country when you register
  • Usage Data: Chat activity, login times, pages visited (anonymous)
  • Device Data: Browser type, device info, IP address (for security)
  • Messages: Content of your chats (stored temporarily, not monitored)

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data helps us provide and improve our services:

  • Connect you with other users for chatting
  • Authenticate your identity and maintain your account
  • Improve our platform based on usage patterns
  • Protect against fraud and abuse
  • Send important account updates (opt-out available)

3. Data Storage & Security

We take security seriously:

  • Passwords are encrypted using industry-standard methods
  • Chat messages are not stored permanently (except with friends)
  • We use HTTPS to encrypt all data in transit
  • Regular security audits help keep our systems safe

4. Cookies & Tracking

We use cookies for essential functionality:

  • Essential Cookies: Keep you logged in, remember preferences
  • Analytics: Help us understand how people use our site (anonymous)
  • You can disable cookies in your browser settings

5. Sharing Your Data

We NEVER sell your personal data to third parties.

We may share data only in these cases:

  • Service Providers: Companies that help run our platform (hosting, etc.)
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law or to protect safety
  • With Your Consent: When you explicitly agree

6. Your Rights

You have control over your data:

  • Access: View your account data anytime
  • Edit: Update your profile information
  • Delete: Request account deletion (contact support)
  • Export: Download your data
  • Opt-out: Unsubscribe from promotional emails

7. Children's Privacy

ChatOnline is designed for users 13+:

  • We restrict use to ages 13 and older
  • We don't knowingly collect data from under-13 users
  • Parents can contact us to review or delete their child's data

8. Contact Us

Questions about this policy? Reach out:

  • Email: privacy@chatonline.app
  • Through our Contact Form
